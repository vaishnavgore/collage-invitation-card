Dear Esteemed Colleagues, Faculty & Students,

Engineering is much more than equations and technical frameworks — it is a shared mindset of problem-solving, resilience, and boundless invention.

The Departments of Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning (AIML) and Computer Science & Engineering (Data Science) take immense pride and joy in welcoming every branch of engineering across our campus to celebrate Engineering Day 2026.

Let us gather together at the Seminar Hall on September 15, 2026 to honor our past milestones, present innovations, and the limitless future we are creating together.

Program Itinerary

The Day's Journey

Tuesday, September 15, 2026  ·  01:00 PM – 03:40 PM  ·  Seminar Hall, Samarth Campus

01:00 PM – 01:20 PM Inaugural

Grand Inauguration & Traditional Lamp Lighting

Opening welcome address for all engineering families followed by the auspicious lighting of the ceremonial lamp with college leadership and department heads.

01:20 PM – 01:40 PM Keynote & Address

Engineers' Day Speech & HOD Addresses

Commemorative tribute honoring Bharat Ratna Sir M. Visvesvaraya, followed by inspiring keynote perspectives shared by the Heads of Departments and faculty.

01:40 PM – 01:50 PM Campus Memory

Official Campus Group Photograph

A memorable group photo capturing all engineering families, department HODs, faculty members, and student representatives together.

01:50 PM – 02:20 PM Staff Special

Faculty & Staff Celebrations

Interactive fun activities and special celebrations honoring our esteemed professors and staff across all departments.

02:20 PM – 03:25 PM Student Activities

Inter-Department Student Celebrations

High-spirited team challenges, collaborative fun, and campus-wide camaraderie among students of all engineering disciplines.

03:25 PM – 03:40 PM Grand Finale

Meme Show, Felicitation & Closing Ceremony

Campus engineering meme showcase, felicitation of participants, closing address, and official vote of thanks by the organizing committee.
